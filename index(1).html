Welcome to Bidii High School

The school is located in Nairobi near the city center.

The aim of the school is to raise men of substance and who have values and are ready for the real-world experience.

About Us

Bidii High School is committed to providing quality education and fostering a supportive learning environment for all students. Our dedicated faculty and staff work together to ensure that every student has the opportunity to succeed academically and personally.

The school supports both genders(male and female) and also the environment is friendly to any other genders.

We are committed to providing equal opportunities for all students.

Academics

Our academic programs are designed to challenge and inspire students. We offer a wide range of courses in various subjects, including science, mathematics, literature, and the arts. Our curriculum is continuously updated to meet the evolving needs of our students and prepare them for college and beyond.

We have a history of posting excellent results nationally and internationally.

Admissions

We welcome students from diverse backgrounds to join our school community. Our admissions process is designed to be inclusive and transparent. Prospective students and their families can learn more about our admissions criteria, application process, and important deadlines by visiting our admissions page.
